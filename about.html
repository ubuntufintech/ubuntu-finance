About Ubuntu Finance

Born From Lived Experience

Ubuntu Finance was not born in a boardroom. It was born from the inside of a debt cycle that millions of people around the world are still trapped in today.

The founder's story

A Cycle That Had to End

When I started working in 2014, I earned N$8,000 a month, approximately USD 500. To many people that would have been a meaningful income. But by the time payday came and went, half of it was already gone, swallowed by loan repayments I had committed to without ever truly understanding what I was signing up for.

As my salary increased, so did my access to credit. Every time I finished paying off one loan, the bank would call. The message was always the same: congratulations, you qualify for a larger loan. And I would take it. Every time. Loan after loan, I was not building anything. I was just moving debt from one place to another.

Then came overdrafts, then a credit card approved on the spot, then money borrowed from the people around me. Some months my entire salary was deducted before I could touch it, and the borrowing started again just to get through.

Nobody had taught me anything about money. Not in school. Not at home. Not at work. I did not know financial literacy was even a subject, a set of skills that could be learned. And if personal financial coaching existed, it was expensive and inaccessible. The people who needed it most were the ones who could least afford it.

That experience, that cycle of debt, confusion and financial isolation, is why Ubuntu Finance exists.

“If I had Ubuntu Finance in 2014, my financial story would have been completely different. That is not a product claim. It is the reason this platform was built.”

Founder, Ubuntu Finance

The philosophy

What Ubuntu Means to Us

Community Over Individual

Ubuntu means that a person is a person through other people. True financial freedom is not a solo journey; it is something we build together, as a community.

No One Left Behind

When one person in a community is struggling financially, it is not just their problem; it is a collective one. Ubuntu Finance exists to break cycles, not perpetuate them.

Dignity in Every Transaction

Every person deserves to manage their money with dignity: no predatory fees, no hidden charges, no exploitation of those who have the least.

Lifting as We Climb

When someone finds their financial footing here, they lift others with them, through lending, through knowledge, through community.

Our vision

A secure, intelligent and borderless financial platform that gives people the power to transact, borrow, manage and grow their money with trust and dignity. A One World Digital Bank that makes physical branches unnecessary.

Our mission

To protect users, improve access to essential financial services and empower communities through transparent, ethical, technology-driven finance, combining AI, Machine Learning and Blockchain with a deep commitment to the people we serve.
