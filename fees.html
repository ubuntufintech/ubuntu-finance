No penalty for being short

We never charge a failed-payment or below-balance fee. If the money is not there, the payment simply does not go, and we tell you why.

No compounding, ever

A salary advance costs 5% of what you draw. Not 5% a month, not 5% that grows. One fee, shown before you accept, repaid on payday.

No fee you have not seen

Every charge appears on the confirmation screen before you approve, and on the transaction record afterwards with the fee line spelled out.

Who holds your money

Deposits and card issuing are provided through our regulated Banking-as-a-Service partner bank under their licence. Ubuntu Finance operates the technology, the AI and the ledger. Your funds are held in a segregated client account, separate from company money, and every movement is written to a tamper-proof record you can inspect on any transaction.
