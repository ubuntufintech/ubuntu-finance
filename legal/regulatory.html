Regulatory Information

Last updated 18 August 2026. Written in plain language on purpose.

Who holds your money

Deposits and card issuing are provided through regulated partner institutions under their licences. Ubuntu Finance operates the technology, the AI and the ledger.

Segregation

Client funds are held in a segregated client account, separate from company money.

Per-market licensing

Licence numbers and partner names are published per country and updated whenever a partnership changes. Ubuntu Finance does not offer deposit-taking or lending services in a market before the relevant partnership and permissions are in place.

Lending

Gateway is a peer-to-peer marketplace. Interest rates are capped at 20% and every agreement is recorded with a tamper-proof hash.

Current status

This build is a sandbox environment. Accounts and records are real; balances are simulated test funds and no live money movement takes place.
