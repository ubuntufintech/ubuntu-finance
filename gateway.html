How it works

For Lenders and Borrowers

For Lenders

Set your own terms and earn returns by lending to verified borrowers anywhere in the world. Before you lend, review the borrower’s credit record and repayment history.

  • Browse borrowers from anywhere in the world
  • Review credit scores and repayment history
  • Set interest rates: 15% standard, up to 20% urgent
  • Blockchain-backed agreement, tamper-proof and enforceable
  • Repayment deducted directly from the borrower’s account

For Borrowers

Access fair credit from individual lenders worldwide. Your borrowing limit is determined by your verified income and credit score. The higher your score, the more you can access.

  • Borrowing limit based on verified income and credit score
  • Clear repayment terms shown before you accept
  • Interest rate tied to urgency, capped at 20%
  • Must accept all stated conditions before confirmation
  • Repayments auto-deducted from your linked account
